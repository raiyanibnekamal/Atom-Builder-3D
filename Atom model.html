Did You Know?
P⁺

PROTON

A proton carries a positive charge and lives inside the nucleus. The number of protons tells us which element the atom is — it's the atom's identity!

⚡Charge: +1 (positive)
⚖️Mass: ~1 atomic mass unit (amu)
📍Location: Inside the nucleus
🔢Proton count = Atomic Number (Z)
🎨Symbol: p or p⁺
N⁰

NEUTRON

Neutrons have no electric charge — they are neutral! They live in the nucleus beside protons and help hold the nucleus together with nuclear force.

⚡Charge: 0 (neutral)
⚖️Mass: ~1 atomic mass unit (amu)
📍Location: Inside the nucleus
🔢Neutrons = Mass Number − Atomic Number
🎨Symbol: n or n⁰
e⁻

ELECTRON

Electrons are tiny, negatively charged particles that orbit the nucleus in shells. They are responsible for chemical reactions and electricity!

⚡Charge: −1 (negative)
⚖️Mass: almost zero (negligible)
📍Location: Shells around the nucleus
🏠Shell 1: max 2 · Shell 2: max 8 · Shell 3: max 8
🎨Symbol: e⁻
